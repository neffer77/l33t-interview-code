# R9 — Deep Economy & Supply Chains

R9 makes P2 concept resources part of the live city simulation.

## Loop

1. Interview solves import concept-specific resources.
2. Connected buildings transform those resources every 30 seconds of active city time.
3. Roads act as logistics links; disconnected producers stop.
4. Recipes consume inputs and operating budget, create downstream resources, and sometimes generate trade revenue.
5. Population contributes modest tax revenue while R7 maintenance continues to pressure the budget.
6. Input, logistics, or budget shortages reduce real building output/service capacity.
7. Players can pause production or toggle the flow overlay.

## Chain

- Array Foundry: budget → Materials
- Hash Market: Materials → Trade + revenue
- Search Observatory: Trade → Research
- DP Research Lab: Research → Compute
- Solar Array: Compute → Infrastructure
- Graph Transit Hub: Infrastructure + Trade → Stability + revenue
- Interview Academy: Trade + Stability → Research
- Housing and Recovery Park periodically consume city supplies.

Coding remains the primary import system: Arrays/Strings → Materials, Hash/Structures → Trade, Trees/Graphs/Search → Research, DP/Optimization → Compute, Systems → Infrastructure, Reliability/Security → Stability.

## Player-visible acceptance

The City view has an Economy panel showing stockpiles, budget, tax forecast, recipes, and shortages. The map shows FLOW markers plus SUPPLY, LOGISTICS, and BUDGET warnings, and successful production creates resource bursts over buildings.

Economic failure can hurt city throughput but never deletes solved problems, mastery, retention, interview readiness, or other learning progress.
