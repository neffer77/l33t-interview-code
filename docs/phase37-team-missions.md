# Phase 37 — NPC Team Missions

Phase 37 turns persistent NPC relationships into collaborative engineering gameplay.

## Goals

- Make relationships matter in play without turning them into score multipliers.
- Add paired missions that combine coordination choices with real technical evidence.
- Preserve the authority of existing coding, incident, design, review, project, and behavioral systems.

## Mission flow

1. Reach at least **Working Rapport** with an NPC.
2. Receive a deterministic mission aligned with that teammate's specialty.
3. Choose how to divide responsibility or coordinate the work.
4. Launch the existing authoritative activity.
5. Complete the real judged/scored work.
6. Archive the collaboration and rotate to another eligible mission.

## Initial mission set

- Pair on a Hot Path — Maya + judged mastery
- Production Pair Response — Theo + incident response
- Architecture Review Council — Jin + system design
- Ship the Automation — Luna + engineering project
- Repository Review Rotation — Theo + repository review
- Stakeholder Alignment Drill — Marcus + behavioral evidence

## Integrity boundary

NPCs provide framing, coordination pressure, and narrative context. They never satisfy hidden tests, complete rubric evidence, modify scores, or bypass required player work.

## Persistence

Active mission, coordination choice, and recent mission history are stored locally under `codeopolis-phase37-team-missions-v1`.
