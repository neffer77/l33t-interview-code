# Phase 30 — Adaptive Interview Remediation

Phase 30 closes the loop between realistic Interview Day calibration and targeted learning.

## Core loop

1. Complete a Phase 29 Interview Day.
2. Identify the lowest-scoring measured interview signal.
3. Generate a short remediation contract using existing authoritative Codeopolis activities.
4. Produce fresh scored evidence in the weak signal.
5. Re-run the same company Interview Day.
6. Clear remediation only when both the company calibration bar and the repaired signal target are met.

## Signal-specific repair

- **Coding** — a fresh hidden-test solve plus a scored technical explanation of that solution.
- **Technical reasoning** — a fresh scored explanation plus a judged implementation.
- **Production incident** — a scored incident simulation at 75+.
- **Debugging** — a resolved debugging scenario with the existing authoritative resolver.
- **System design** — a design simulation at 75+.
- **Behavioral** — a behavioral response at 75+.

The final re-test uses the same company profile as the source mock. A high overall score cannot hide the original weakness: the repaired signal must also clear its own target.

## Automatic vs optional plans

A `Below Bar` Interview Day automatically creates remediation when no plan is already active. Successful mock days do not create mandatory work; the player may manually generate a plan from the latest completed mock if they want to sharpen the weakest remaining signal.

## Progression integrity

There are no manual completion checkboxes. Phase 30 listens to the existing mastery, interviewer, incident, debugging, design, behavioral, and Interview Day events. It does not replace or weaken any grader.

Remediation completion does not directly grant hiring offers, employment, promotions, or interview scores. It is coaching evidence and learning structure layered on top of the existing systems.

## UX

The Adaptive Remediation panel sits next to Interview Day and shows:

- source company and weak score;
- repaired signal;
- target for each step;
- completed evidence score;
- overall remediation progress;
- direct launch controls for the relevant existing simulator;
- final re-test requirements.

This turns the mock-interview debrief into a concrete next action instead of a static score report.
