# Phase 12 — Civilization Legacy

Phase 12 gives Codeopolis a long-term endgame without turning retention into empty grinding.

## Mastery collection

Relics unlock only from demonstrated progress such as durable district mastery, broad Knowledge Index, high mock scores, system-design completion, incident work, mentor relationships, hidden discoveries, and Principal-level readiness.

The initial collection contains 12 permanent relics. Locked relics are partially hidden so progression retains discovery and anticipation without random loot boxes.

## Learning-first achievements

The initial 12 achievements reward useful learning behavior: judged solves, breadth, independent recall, recovering from failed tests, strong mocks, reasoning quality, system design, production incidents, mentor growth, career progression, and retained knowledge.

There are deliberately no achievements for opening the app on consecutive days.

## Prestige / New Game+

Prestige becomes available only after a broad endgame profile:

- Knowledge Index 70+
- Principal Engineer career rank
- 48+ distinct solved problems
- 6+ permanent relics
- 8+ learning achievements

Prestige preserves solved problems, mastery and review history, career/mock records, mentor relationships, achievements, collections, and engineering interview/design history.

Prestige resets rebuildable civilization state such as credits, research, buildings, roads, purchased technologies, doctrine, eras, bosses, crises, and daily civic progression.

A pre-prestige backup is taken through the Phase 11 save-safety system before the reset.

Each completed legacy grants a permanent +5% solve reward bonus, capped at +25%. The bonus is intentionally modest so knowledge remains the dominant progression mechanism.

## Design principle

The purpose of New Game+ is to make mastery feel permanent while making the civilization replayable. The player should never be asked to erase evidence of learning in exchange for game progression.
