# Phase 34 — Civilization Eras, Victory Paths, and Legacy

Phase 34 gives Codeopolis a long-horizon civilization endgame without turning replayability into a destructive prestige reset.

## Civilization eras

Era progress is derived from demonstrated city development: district levels, landmarks, retained knowledge, engineering projects, repository reviews, and civic health. The initial progression is Settlement → Expansion → Systems → Metropolis → Legacy.

Era changes are presentation/progression milestones only. They do not lower interview difficulty, alter hidden tests, or grant mastery.

## Victory paths

Four civilization victories emphasize different evidence mixes:

- **Knowledge Civilization** — Knowledge Quarter level 4, retained skills, Grand Algorithm Archive, and strong Innovation.
- **Resilient Civilization** — Resilience Ward level 4, repository reviews, engineering projects, and strong Stability.
- **Autonomous Civilization** — Automation Works level 4, engineering projects, Autonomous Civic Core, and strong Capacity.
- **Civic Civilization** — Civic Forum level 4, Interview Day evidence, leadership signals, and strong Public Trust.

The intent is to create distinct strategic goals without making one path the canonical correct ending.

## Legacy archive

After completing a victory, the player can record a civilization legacy and choose a future doctrine: Exploration, Stewardship, Craft, or Teaching. Legacy records capture the victory, doctrine, era, charter, and date.

Recording a legacy is **non-destructive**. It does not reset or erase:

- judged challenge mastery;
- retained-skill evidence;
- interview history or remediation;
- engineering projects or repository reviews;
- career or leadership evidence;
- city districts, landmarks, or civic decision history.

This makes replayability additive: the player can pursue another victory identity or doctrine using the skills they genuinely learned instead of being asked to re-grind already demonstrated knowledge.

## Authority boundary

Civilization victories and legacies are game-layer accomplishments. They cannot mark technical work correct, satisfy interview rubrics, create job offers, or modify authoritative learning scores.
