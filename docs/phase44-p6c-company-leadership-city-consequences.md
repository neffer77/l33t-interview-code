# Phase 44 P6-C — Company & Leadership Systems → City Consequences

P6-C connects existing company, technical-leadership, and organizational-influence events to the civilization without replacing those legacy systems.

## City consequence state

The integration persists a bounded `companyCity` model with four values:

- `culture` — collaboration and organizational health
- `execution` — ability to turn decisions into delivery
- `influence` — reach of technical/organizational decisions
- `alignment` — derived average across the three primary values

All values are clamped to 0–100. They are consequence signals, not a second economy.

## Event mapping

- `company:event` emphasizes culture and execution.
- `leadership:event` emphasizes execution and influence.
- `organization:event` emphasizes organizational influence.

The P6-A bridge remains the canonical adapter. P6-C listens to normalized `existing-system:company` activity and reuses Living City reactions so important company milestones are visible in the civilization.

## Design rule

Existing Company Life, Technical Leadership, and Organization Influence systems remain authoritative. P6-C only projects their consequences into city state and presentation.
