# Phase 13 — Career World

Phase 13 turns the linear career ladder into a world of specialization, reputation, and adaptive contracts.

## Career paths

Players choose one persistent specialization: Technical Leadership, AI Engineering, Infrastructure, Security, or Startup. Each path emphasizes different mastery districts, exposes its own role ladder, and provides a modest civilization production bias.

Career advancement requires demonstrated mastery in the path's focus areas plus solved-problem breadth. It cannot be purchased with credits.

## Rival organizations

Nova Robotics, Atlas Cloud, Helix AI, Vector Security, and Orbital Systems now exist as persistent organizations with separate reputation. Reputation grows by delivering work, not by passive timers.

## World contracts

Organizations generate contracts around their specialties and the player's weaker relevant mastery areas. Contracts point to real judged coding challenges. Completing the challenge through the normal hidden-test judge pays credits/research and increases organization reputation.

## Design rule

Career identity should increase agency and replayability without fragmenting the learning curriculum. All paths still reward durable technical skill and use the same verified judge as the source of truth.
