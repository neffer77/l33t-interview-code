# Phase 32 — Civilization Specialization

Phase 32 deepens the civilization side of Codeopolis so learning evidence changes what the city becomes, not just the numbers at the top of the screen.

## Civilization charters

Players choose one long-term identity:

- Research Metropolis
- Resilient Grid
- Automation Capital
- Civic Commons

A charter changes progression flavor and city identity. It does not change hidden tests, interview rubrics, or authoritative learning scores.

## Evidence-driven districts

Four persistent districts evolve from real game evidence:

- Knowledge Quarter — mastery, retention, and engineering projects
- Resilience Ward — repository reviews, projects, and leadership
- Automation Works — projects, mastery, and review evidence
- Civic Forum — interview calibration, leadership, and retained knowledge

Districts progress through four visible levels. Evolution is triggered by scored/mastered events rather than passive timers.

## Landmark goals

Long-term mixed evidence unlocks landmarks including the Grand Algorithm Archive, Engineering Exchange, Systems Spire, and Autonomous Civic Core. These are intended as durable civilization milestones that require multiple kinds of demonstrated engineering capability.

## Design boundary

Phase 32 makes the game layer more meaningful without turning city choices into grading exploits. Civilization specialization can provide identity, visual progression, and future economic/perk hooks, while all learning outcomes remain controlled by the existing judges and rubrics.
