# R13 — Interview Campaign Integration

R13 turns the reconstructed city into the primary interview-prep campaign surface. Interview problems are no longer primarily detached exercises that happen to award city resources; the city itself presents the reasons to practice.

## City-first mission loop

1. Inspect Codeopolis.
2. Open **Interview Campaign** or follow an in-world mission beacon.
3. Choose one of the current city needs.
4. The campaign maps that need to an adaptive interview challenge.
5. The full coding workspace opens with the city origin and reason preserved.
6. A valid progression-eligible solve returns to the City view.
7. The exact originating building/tile is reselected and the camera refocuses there.
8. Existing city systems apply the real consequence: repair, crisis recovery, learning-contract progress, imported resources, or readiness progression.

The campaign intentionally routes existing R7 repairs, R11 crises, and P3 learning contracts through their established resolution flows rather than inventing parallel repair/crisis systems.

## Mission priority

The mission board derives work from the live physical simulation:

- active systemic crises,
- degraded/outage buildings,
- road and power disconnections,
- R9 supply-chain shortages/logistics failures,
- locked learning buildings,
- and the weakest concept needed for the current interview-readiness milestone.

The board surfaces multiple choices so the player keeps agency instead of receiving a single mandatory exercise.

## Finite readiness campaign

The interview campaign has five achievable milestones:

1. **Foundation Screen Ready** — 20% readiness, Age II, 1 verified concept.
2. **Core Patterns Ready** — 35% readiness, Age III, 2 verified concepts.
3. **Algorithm Round Ready** — 50% readiness, Age IV, 4 verified concepts.
4. **Onsite Loop Ready** — 68% readiness, Age V, 5 verified concepts.
5. **Interview Ready** — 82% readiness, Age V+, 6 verified concepts, no more than one false-mastery flag.

These thresholds reuse the existing P2 InterviewReadiness model, which already combines mastery, retention, transfer/generalization, verified clean solves, quality and interleaving.

Completing the final milestone does **not** end or reset the city. Codeopolis switches to endless mode: city building, expansion, optimization, crises, economy and continued interview practice remain available forever.

## World presentation

The top three campaign needs receive in-world beacons over their originating buildings. The highest-priority need is visually emphasized. An active generic campaign mission pulses at its origin. After the final milestone, an **INTERVIEW READY · CITY CONTINUES** badge appears at the academy (or another city building if no academy exists).

## Safety

Campaign completion and city failure never delete solved problems, concept mastery, retention, transfer, technologies, readiness history or other earned learning state.

## Acceptance boundary

R13 is code-complete only when regression and syntax validation pass. The Product Constitution still requires deployed mobile/desktop player-visible observation before the reconstructed city-facing roadmap can be called visually accepted.
