# Phase 21 — Organization Influence

Phase 21 extends the Staff/Principal simulation from individual leadership exercises into multi-team execution.

## Core loop

Executive objective → decompose into workstreams → delegate to NPC owners → review delegated technical plans → resolve a cross-team dependency → create a talent-development plan → defend the integrated architecture → receive an organization-impact score.

## Learning invariant

Organization progress is evidence-producing rather than click-producing. Starting a program, assigning an owner, or opening a simulator does not complete the program. Written reviews and decisions must clear explicit local rubrics, and the final execution gate reuses the existing scored system-design lab.

## Eligibility

A player must have accepted employment, delivered at least three projects at that employer, and completed a 75+ Phase 20 leadership program.

## Organization Impact Score

The persistent impact model exposes four dimensions: technical direction, delegation quality, cross-team execution, and talent development. Completed programs update these dimensions from their measured evidence.

## Delegation

Each program has three workstreams. NPC specialty and earned trust improve delegation quality; assigning the same person to multiple workstreams creates an overload penalty. Delegation itself grants no mastery or promotion evidence.

## Review instead of rewrite

Each delegated workstream requires a written review covering correctness/invariants, complexity, validation, risk/blast radius, and an actionable recommendation. This tests recognition and senior review judgment rather than requiring the player to personally implement every task.

## Cross-team dependencies

The dependency decision rubric requires a decision, tradeoffs, ownership, migration/sequencing, safety/reversibility, and measurement. This models the coordination work that becomes central at Staff/Principal scope.

## NPC growth

Owners of successfully delivered workstreams gain persistent capability after the program completes. Growth is downstream of validated delivery, so repeatedly asking for advice or assigning work cannot farm teammate progression.

## No waiting mechanics

Programs contain no real-world timers, energy gates, or deadline punishment. Difficulty comes from technical judgment and evidence quality rather than waiting.
