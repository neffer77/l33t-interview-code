# Phase 44 P6-D — Social & Team Systems → City Consequences

P6-D projects existing NPC relationship, team mission, and squad project activity into the civilization without replacing those source systems.

## Persistent city signals

- `cohesion` — social trust and team connectedness.
- `mentorship` — strength of teaching and guidance relationships.
- `collaboration` — ability to work effectively across citizens/teams.
- `momentum` — forward progress generated by team missions and projects.
- `synergy` — derived average of the four primary signals.

All signals are clamped to `0–100`. They are consequence/readout state, not a second currency or progression economy.

## Event mapping

- `npc:relationship` emphasizes cohesion and mentorship.
- `team:mission` emphasizes collaboration and momentum.
- `squad:project` emphasizes project momentum and collaboration.

The P6-A bridge remains the canonical event adapter. P6-D listens to normalized `existing-system:social` activity and reuses Living City reactions for visible feedback.

## Design constraint

NPC Relationships, Team Missions, Squad Projects, and AI-character systems remain authoritative. P6-D only reflects their outcomes into the city so social/team progress becomes visible in the civilization.
