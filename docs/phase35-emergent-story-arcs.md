# Phase 35 — Emergent Story Arcs

Phase 35 turns the civilization into an ongoing narrative campaign layer.

## Goal

Make the player feel that engineering practice is happening inside a living city rather than in disconnected menus. Campaigns are generated from persistent civilization context and then completed through existing authoritative game activities.

## Campaign selection

The active Phase 32 charter selects the broad campaign family:

- Research Metropolis → Frontier Discovery Program
- Resilient Grid → Grid Under Siege
- Automation Capital → Autonomous Expansion
- Civic Commons → Civic Compact

Campaign metadata also records the weakest current district, current era, and civic health at generation time so the arc reflects the player's actual civilization state.

## Chapter evidence

Campaign chapters route into existing systems such as judged coding, technical reasoning, repository review, engineering projects, incidents, debugging, system design, behavioral evaluation, and Interview Day.

Opening a chapter or navigating to an activity does not complete it. A chapter advances only when the corresponding authoritative completion event fires.

## Progression boundary

Campaigns are a narrative/composition layer. They do not replace hidden tests or scoring rubrics, and they do not independently award mastery, retained status, interview outcomes, career state, or leadership progression.

## Replayability

Completed arcs are archived locally. A new campaign is generated after completion using the current civilization charter/state, allowing the narrative to evolve as the city changes without requiring destructive resets.
